About the numbers

Issue 2.3. Figures from the Billy Button dashboard build of Sat 7 Nov 26, 3:06 am AEDT. The report is cut each Saturday morning from the overnight build, which closes the Monday-to-Friday week; nothing entered after 6 pm Friday counts. Forecast = average of the last 13 full weeks (weeks of 3 Aug to 26 Oct), the three-month rule. Solid dot = this week; ring = last week; green = moved from forecast toward target, brown = moved away.

How each measure is counted — one line each, in the order shown, using the dashboard's terms, which are LineLeader's and Owna's own words where the data is theirs. The base case is a snapshot: occupancy now, less leavers, plus the two queues, equals the book at end February; month cells show children with their spots. The performance rows are weekly rates against the forecast and target, with last week as the hollow ring. Colours: brown below forecast, amber between forecast and target, green at or beyond target.

Occupancy
All Owna booked child-days Mon–Fri, casual days included, ÷ licensed places × 5. The sub-line shows the casual days inside the total.
− Leaving for school
Children whose Owna record says they start school in 2027, by the month of their Owna finish date. "No date" = no finish date entered yet. The forecast assumes every leaver has gone by the end of January; last season at South Morang 40% of leaver spots went in December, 44% in January, 16% in February.
+ Enrolled, not started
Children with an Owna enrolment whose start date is after the snapshot, by start month, with their booked days a week as the spots they add. Last week as at Fri 30 Oct. The byline shows pulled in and started out.
+ Pending
Families whose Owna enrolment form is in but has not yet been pulled through (Owna shows them as Pending), by the start month the parent gave. "Past start" = that month has passed and the child is still not in Owna. Families more than 60 days past the step's target are gone quiet (the dashboard's term: mostly lost, not yet closed in LineLeader); they are named in the byline and left out of the count and the base case. Pending is not yet a start: of the Jan–Aug 26 cohorts, forms that went on to a start were 25 of 32 at South Morang (78%) and 43 of 66 at Craigieburn (65%).
= Base case, end Feb 27
Occupancy now, less the school leavers (all assumed gone by end January), plus due-to-start and pending spots (most start by February), ÷ licensed places × 5. Where each centre lands if nothing else changes: no new enquiries, no new losses, every pending family starts. The gap from here to the forecast and target is what the flow below has to deliver.
New enquiries
New LineLeader enquiries in the week, counted in families (the dashboard's certified weekly metric).
Tours completed
Tours that took place in the week, counted in families (certified weekly metric).
Enrolment complete
Children pulled into Owna in the week: room, days and compliance confirmed, the account created and the $50 charged. This is the conversion the forecast is built on.
Started
Booked days a week of children whose Owna start date fell in the week. Same children as "Enrolled, not started", counted the week they walk in. Extra days added by existing children are not in it.
Lost spots
Booked days a week of Owna formal withdrawals by finish week. School leavers are shown separately; day reductions are not included.

Targets are weekly rates: occupancy at 30 Jun 27 (South Morang 85%, Craigieburn 70%); spots gained from the Target chain in children × average days (South Morang 7, Craigieburn 33 a week); spots lost 9 a month per centre; enquiries, tours and enrolled from the model's Target chain (South Morang 31 enquiries a month, Craigieburn 150, at certified conversion). Pending, due to start and school leavers are the forward book and carry no target.

About the numbers

Every line counts families who met the rule, so up is good: green arrow = more than the comparison, red = fewer, orange = within 10% either side. This wk is the week ending Fri 6 Nov 26; last wk ends 30 Oct, 4 wks ends 9 Oct. All from the dashboard's rule findings (when each miss was first detected) against the week's enquiries and tours.

First contact within 10 min: new enquiries in the week whose first human contact — a person's message, a logged call or a completed call task, never LineLeader's automatic acknowledgement — came within 10 minutes of the family record being created (dashboard view first_contact). The rule is 10 minutes; it carries no forecast or target. A call that is not logged does not count.

Tours kept: the dashboard's show rate — booked tours whose day has passed less those recorded Tour Not Completed. Offer made at the tour: moved to Offered in LineLeader within two hours of the tour. Tour outcome recorded: tours booked less those still with no outcome entered. Tour followed up, 2 days: tours booked less those with no call, text or email within 2 business days. No-show followed up: tours recorded Tour Not Completed less those not contacted.

Follow-ups cleared: families whose stalled flag was cleared in the week because the next step happened. The line above the list is the other side of it: stalled (waiting on us) at the close of the week (stalled at a stage), with the two earlier readings. That one should fall.

Where a miss is detected after the week closes it counts in the week detected, so recent weeks can firm up for a week or two. Names are never shown here; each line has its own list on the dashboard's Follow-ups screen.

About the numbers

Window is the 12 months to the issue date. The trend is a rolling three months, month by month; the open dot is the latest, which always reads low because enrolment runs 10 to 50 days behind the tour. Line and dots are earth below 40%, amber 40 to 80%, sage at 80% and above.

Where 40% and 80% come from: LineLeader, 2026 ECE Benchmark Report (data from about 5,700 childcare operators on LineLeader). On the industry rate it says post-tour conversion is "trending toward 40%". On the top tier it defines the best-performing centres as those converting 79% or more of completed tours; we round that to 80% and set it as the mark for every tour giver.

Tour to Enrolled (lead card and trend): of every family marked Tour Completed in LineLeader, the share that moved to Enrolled on or after it. Every family tours before enrolling, so every Tour Completed counts, including those recorded later rather than on the day. A family who toured, was never recorded and didn't enrol can't be counted; outcomes are recorded for 90% or more of booked tours, so that gap is small. Each family counts once (dashboard view tour_giver, status runs included).

Record keeping: the line is the last three months, with the trend as a rolling three months by month; the dashed line is 90%, which is where a kept record should sit. Sage at 90% and above, amber 70 to 90, earth below 70. Tour recorded on the day: of families marked Tour Completed, the share with a tour entry or Tour task made at the time. The rest were marked Tour Completed later, usually in a run of clicks at enrolment (the dashboard calls these status runs); they count in the centre rate but carry no name, so they can't be credited to a tour giver.

Tour givers: tours recorded on the day only, because only those name a person. The staff member on the tour's own LineLeader record (the tour entry, else who completed the Tour task, else its assignee). First names from the dashboard's released staff list; a line needs five families in the window. The arrow compares the last six months with the six before, where each has five or more families. "Last tour" shows when a giver hasn't toured in the last two months.

Offered within 2 days of Tour Completed: of tours recorded on the day, the share where the family moved to Offered (or straight to Orientation or Enrolled) within two days. Tours recorded later are left out because their date is the catch-up, not the tour. LineLeader's status history starts 28 Jan 26, so the trend begins in February. There is no published benchmark; Billy shows the trend only until we set a target. Offered on the day, under each tour giver, is the same on the tour's own day.
